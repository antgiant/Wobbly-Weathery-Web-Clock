Where is your sky?

The clock draws the real sky above you: the sun, moon and stars as they are right now. How should it find where you are?

Sunrise & sunset

Appears shortly before the sun reaches the horizon and counts down to it.

Whatever you choose, the options above pick the morning event (shown as "sunrise") and the evening one ("sunset"). Golden hour counts down to its start: the sun 4° below the horizon in the morning, 6° above it in the evening.

Night

A veil that eases in as the sun goes down and out again at dawn, so a bedside clock does not light the room.
